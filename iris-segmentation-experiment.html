Standalone test — not part of PDGO, not deployed there
Experimental — likely mismatch, tested here on purpose This uses a real pretrained model (Worldcoin's open-iris semantic segmentation network), but it was trained ENTIRELY on infrared iris-recognition images (CASIA-Iris, IITD, ND-IRIS, LivDet-Iris) captured on dedicated biometric hardware at close range — not ordinary visible-light phone selfies. The model's own authors document two specific weaknesses: (1) poor results on images with strong specular reflection from glasses, and (2) poor results on off-gaze, misaligned, or blurry images — i.e. normal selfie conditions. This page exists to actually see what happens, not to promise it'll work.
