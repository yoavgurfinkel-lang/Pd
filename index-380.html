PD range correction
Affects Calculated PD and Mono R/L. Leaves everything up to 65mm completely untouched, then applies a small upward touch above 65. The low side is switched off — small PDs pass through unchanged, since no measured error was found there. Deliberately gentle: it closes about half the gap at high PD rather than all of it, because a correction steep enough to fully reach 70 would badly overshoot beyond 72. Fitted on three subjects (57, 64, 70), two of them single shots; adjustment clamped to 2.5mm. Off = the raw blended number (compare the two on any patient with a known PD).
